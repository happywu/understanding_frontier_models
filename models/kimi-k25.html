👁️ Vision Architecture

NaViT patch packing, MoonViT-3D unified encoder, MLA attention, MoE routing

▼

NaViT: Native Resolution Vision Processing

Traditional ViTs resize all images to fixed resolution, losing aspect ratio and wasting computation on padding. NaViT (Native Resolution ViT) uses Patch n' Pack to process images at their native resolution.

How NaViT Processes Variable-Resolution Images

Factorized Positional Embeddings: $$\text{pos\_embed}(\text{patch}) = \text{embed}_x(x_{\text{frac}}) + \text{embed}_y(y_{\text{frac}})$$ where $x_{\text{frac}} = \frac{\text{patch\_col}}{\text{total\_cols}} \in [0, 1]$

MoonViT-3D: Unified Image & Video Processing

MoonViT-3D extends NaViT to video by treating 4 consecutive frames as a spatiotemporal volume, achieving 4x temporal compression through patch-level averaging.

$$\mathbf{p}_{i,j} = \frac{1}{4} \sum_{t=0}^{3} \mathbf{f}_{t,i,j}$$ Patches at same spatial position $(i,j)$ across 4 frames are averaged
Why This Matters: A 60-second video at 30fps has 1,800 frames. With 4x compression, MoonViT-3D processes it as 450 temporal chunks, making long video understanding feasible within the 256K context window.

MLA: Multi-head Latent Attention

The K2 backbone uses Multi-head Latent Attention instead of standard MHA, compressing the KV cache by ~93%.

Standard MHA

Cache full K, V matrices per layer

$\text{Cache} = 2 \times n_h \times d_h$

MLA (K2.5)

Cache compressed latent, reconstruct at attention time

$\text{Cache} = d_c \ll 2 n_h d_h$

$$\mathbf{c} = W_{\text{down}} \cdot \mathbf{h}$$ compress hidden state to latent $$\mathbf{K} = W_{\text{up}}^K \cdot \mathbf{c}, \quad \mathbf{V} = W_{\text{up}}^V \cdot \mathbf{c}$$ reconstruct K, V at attention time

Mixture of Experts (MoE)

K2.5 has 384 experts but activates only 8 per token, enabling 1T+ parameters with 32B compute cost.

$$\mathbf{h}' = \sum_{i \in \text{TopK}(G(\mathbf{h}))} g_i \cdot \text{FFN}_i(\mathbf{h})$$ where $G(\mathbf{h}) = \text{softmax}(W_g \cdot \mathbf{h})$ is the router and $g_i$ are gating weights

🔗 Joint Optimization of Text & Vision

Early fusion, Zero-Vision SFT, cross-modal RL transfer

▼

A key insight: joint optimization enhances both modalities rather than causing conflict.

Early Fusion (10:90 ratio)

Vision tokens included from training start, avoiding the "dip-and-recover" pattern seen when adding vision late.

Zero-Vision SFT

Text-only SFT activates visual reasoning. Human-annotated visual trajectories actually hurt generalization.

Cross-Modal RL Transfer

Visual RL improves text benchmarks: MMLU-Pro +1.7%, GPQA +2.1%, LongBench +2.2%.

Architecture: Trainable Orchestrator + Frozen Sub-Agents

PARL uses a decoupled architecture to avoid credit assignment ambiguity and training instability.

Orchestrator
(Trainable)
Sub-agents: Frozen, parallel

PARL Reward Function

$$r_{\text{PARL}}(x, y) = \lambda_1 \cdot r_{\text{parallel}} + \lambda_2 \cdot r_{\text{finish}} + r_{\text{perf}}(x, y)$$
$r_{\text{parallel}}$

Prevents serial collapse (defaulting to single-agent)

$r_{\text{finish}}$

Prevents spurious parallelism (spawning without purpose)

$r_{\text{perf}}$

Task-level outcome ($\lambda_1, \lambda_2$ annealed to zero)

Critical Steps Metric

Unlike total steps, Critical Steps measures the critical path in the computation graph:

$$\text{CriticalSteps} = \sum_{t=1}^{T} \left( S_{\text{main}}^{(t)} + \max_i S_{\text{sub},i}^{(t)} \right)$$ Incentivizes balanced task decomposition that shortens the longest parallel branch

🎯 Post-Training: SFT & RL

Toggle optimization for 25-30% token reduction, unified agentic RL environment

▼

Toggle: Token-Efficient RL

Alternates between inference-time scaling and budget-constrained optimization:

Phase 0 Budget Limited

Solve within token budget. Constraint enforced when accuracy exceeds threshold.

Phase 1 Standard Scaling

Generate up to max tokens, encouraging inference-time scaling.

Toggle Results: 25-30% output token reduction with negligible performance impact. Redundant patterns (repeated verifications, mechanical calculations) decrease substantially.
